Grand Theft Auto VI is no longer a rumour to verify — it launched on Thursday 19 November 2026 on PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S, after two delays (fall 2025, then 26 May 2026, then November 2026), with a staggered midnight local roll-out starting in New Zealand. The review embargo lifted on 18 November, and the game sits at a Metacritic aggregate of 99/100 from 150+ reviews. Prices are $79.99 for the Standard edition and $99.99 for the Ultimate edition. No PC version has been announced.

## What Rockstar has actually confirmed

Rockstar Games and Take-Two have officially confirmed the framing facts: the protagonists are Jason Duval and Lucia Caminos, and the setting is Vice City, in the fictional state of Leonida. That much comes from the trailers, the official site and store listings.

## The structure numbers are guide-sourced — and they agree

Rockstar itself has never published chapter counts, mission counts or completion-time estimates. The numbers in circulation come from hands-on guides of the released game — GameSpot, GameRant and KeenGamer independently agree exactly: a Prologue plus five chapters — *Just Like Paradise* (22 missions), *Paradise Lost* (11), *Ti… A… Drug Money* (26), *Vice City, Baby* (25) and *Top of the World, Ma* (7) — totalling 91 main story missions.

Two caveats keep that honest. First, the exact total is not fully settled: some guides count 93 missions depending on how mandatory story activities are classified, so "more than 90" is safe while an exact figure is not. Second, the "over 65 hours" figure is GameSpot's own staff estimate — "upwards of 65 hours" for the main campaign, 90–100 hours with exploration, and around 200 hours for 100% completion. Other guides differ: Beebom puts the campaign at roughly 50–65 hours and about 90 missions, and KeenGamer at 60–80 hours.

So the forwarded claim's own hedge — "not independently confirmed" — is right about Rockstar, but it understates the position: the structure is corroborated by multiple major outlets playing the finished game. The accurate summary is: five chapters and 91 missions per the guides, 65+ hours per GameSpot's estimate, and none of it officially confirmed by Rockstar.
