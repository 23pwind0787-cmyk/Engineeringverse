NVIDIA has announced that co-founder Jensen Huang has resigned as president, chief executive officer and a member of the board, effective immediately, ending a 33-year run at the head of the company he founded in 1993.

Colette Kress, NVIDIA's executive vice president and chief financial officer, has agreed to serve as interim CEO while keeping her CFO role. The board has begun a search for a permanent chief executive and will consider both internal and external candidates. It also appointed Harvey C. Jones as the company's first-ever chair of the board.

## Who is Colette Kress?

Kress, 59, has been NVIDIA's CFO since joining the company in September 2013. Before that she spent nearly 25 years in finance roles at leading technology companies, including Cisco and Microsoft. During her tenure she led the finance organisation through NVIDIA's growth from a graphics-chip maker into the dominant supplier of AI hardware.

"Collette has helped lead NVIDIA through a period of extraordinary growth and has a deep understanding of our business, customers, operations and financial position," said Harvey Jones, chair of the NVIDIA board. "We expect the company's operations, customer commitments and product roadmap to continue without interruption."

Kress said she was committed to building on "the extraordinary foundation Jensen created over the last 33 years" and to continuing the work that made NVIDIA a leader in accelerated computing and AI.

## What the company said — and what it did not

NVIDIA's statement gives no reason for the resignation. It thanks Huang for his "immeasurable contributions to the company and the industry" and says nothing more about why he left. Reporting in The Wall Street Journal and Le Monde links the departure to personal controversies: the Journal reported on Wednesday that the board was preparing to oust Huang, and Huang acknowledged in a letter to staff on Sunday 8 November that he had been unfaithful to his wife.

The reports concern two relationships. The New York Times first reported in October 2025 on a business connection between Huang and Alice Huang, a Chinese businesswoman associated with Megaspeed, a Singapore-based NVIDIA customer; according to the reporting, US intelligence officials concluded she was providing information to Beijing, a claim China has denied. Le Monde reports that Huang admitted Alice Huang was his mistress. The Wall Street Journal also reported a relationship with Kimberly Powell, NVIDIA's vice president of healthcare. The board launched an independent investigation earlier this month. NVIDIA has not commented beyond its statement announcing the resignation, and no finding that company secrets were stolen has been published.

## Market reaction and what happens next

NVIDIA shares fell sharply on Thursday, dropping as much as 14% during the day to their lowest in nearly 16 months before closing down 4.3%, according to Reuters. Huang remains NVIDIA's fourth-largest shareholder, with a stake of about 3.61%, but will no longer sit on the board.

Among the possible successors, NVIDIA executive Ian Buck — who helped launch AI on NVIDIA chips and led development of the CUDA software system — has emerged as a leading internal candidate, according to Reuters. US President Donald Trump weighed in on social media, writing: "NOW THAT Nvidia CEO JENSEN HUANG, who has done a fantastic job, has resigned, there is only one man to take his place – TIM COOK of Apple. A Legend replaces a Legend!"

NVIDIA will report its third-quarter fiscal 2027 results on Tuesday, November 17, as previously scheduled, followed by its earnings call — the first results under the new interim leadership, five days after the announcement.
