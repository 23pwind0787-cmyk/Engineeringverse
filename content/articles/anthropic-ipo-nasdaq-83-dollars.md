Some claims deserve suspicion, and this one got it — Anthropic was, until this week, a private company. But the claim checks out against Reuters, Bloomberg, CNBC, CNN, AP and SEC filings: Anthropic has completed its IPO, and it is the largest in US history.

## The numbers, broken down properly

Anthropic confidentially filed its S-1 on 1 June 2026, priced the offering on 18 November 2026, and began trading on the Nasdaq under the ticker ANT on 19 November 2026. The marketed range was $70–78; the final price was $83.00. Shares opened at $111.50, up 34%, before pulling back to about $94 in the first session.

- **The IPO raised about $81.3 billion** — 980 million Class A shares at $83, of which about 956 million were sold by Anthropic itself for roughly $79.4 billion, the rest by selling stockholders. That passes SpaceX's $75 billion debut earlier in 2026 as the largest US IPO ever.
- **A separate private investment of about $26 billion** from Nvidia, Google, Microsoft and others brings the combined total to roughly $107.3 billion. So "over $100 billion through its IPO and planned private placements" is accurate — as long as the breakdown stays visible, because the IPO alone is $81.3 billion of it.
- **Valuation:** coverage puts it at roughly $1.5 trillion at the $83 price. One nuance worth a line: on shares outstanding alone, $83 implies about $1.10 trillion of market capitalisation, and about $1.41 trillion fully diluted per the prospectus share count — $1.5 trillion is the headline figure used across the coverage.

## Amodei's safety pledge

In his speech ahead of the Nasdaq opening bell, CEO Dario Amodei reaffirmed the company's safety commitment, pledging that commercial and political pressure would not dictate its development schedule: "We will pace the training and deployment of AI systems, taking as long as is necessary to have confidence that they are safe and will remain under human control."

## How fast this happened

The pre-IPO context underlines the pace. Anthropic's last private round was the Series H in May 2026 — $65 billion at a $965 billion post-money valuation, led by Altimeter, Dragoneer, Greenoaks and Sequoia — following the Series G in February 2026 ($30 billion at $380 billion). Anthropic also committed 5% of its shares to a new Anthropic Public Benefit Fund, announced on 18 October 2026. A company valued at $380 billion in February is now a public company priced at a headline $1.5 trillion in November — whatever one thinks of AI valuations, the fact itself is verified.
