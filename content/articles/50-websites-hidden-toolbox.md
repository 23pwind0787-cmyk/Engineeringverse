## Research and learning

- [Unpaywall](https://unpaywall.org) — free, legal copies of research papers.
- [Open Library](https://openlibrary.org) — borrow books online.
- [DOAJ](https://doaj.org) — the Directory of Open Access Journals, free academic journals.
- [Internet Archive](https://archive.org) — books, media and old websites, archived.
- [Project Gutenberg](https://www.gutenberg.org) — 70,000+ free ebooks.
- [OpenStax](https://openstax.org) — free textbooks.
- [Open Culture](https://openculture.com) — free courses, audiobooks and textbooks.
- [WolframAlpha](https://www.wolframalpha.com) — computational answers to complex problems.
- [Elicit](https://elicit.org) — AI research assistant for finding papers.
- [Consensus](https://consensus.app) — research-backed answers from peer-reviewed papers; its corpus covers over 200 million academic papers.
- [Connected Papers](https://connectedpapers.com) — visual graphs of how research papers connect.
- [Semantic Scholar](https://www.semanticscholar.org) — free academic search.
- [SciSpace](https://scispace.com) — understand research papers faster.
- [Summarize.tech](https://summarize.tech) — AI summaries of long YouTube videos. Note: this one was returning a server error when checked on 11 November 2026 — try again later if it does not load.

## Design, images and video

- [Photopea](https://www.photopea.com) — a free Photoshop alternative that runs in the browser.
- [Squoosh](https://squoosh.app) — compress images without losing much quality.
- [Remove.bg](https://www.remove.bg) — remove image backgrounds automatically.
- [Cleanup.pictures](https://cleanup.pictures) — remove unwanted objects from photos.
- [Unscreen](https://unscreen.com) — remove video backgrounds.
- [Carbon](https://carbon.now.sh) — beautiful images of your code.
- [Ray.so](https://ray.so) — clean code screenshots.
- [Shots](https://shots.so) — product mockups and screenshots.
- [Smartmockups](https://www.smartmockups.com) — create product mockups quickly.

## Privacy and safety

- [Have I Been Pwned](https://haveibeenpwned.com) — check whether your email or password appeared in a data breach.
- [VirusTotal](https://virustotal.com) — scan files and URLs for malware.
- [Privnote](https://privnote.com) — self-destructing notes that delete after being read.
- [Temp Mail](https://temp-mail.org) — a temporary email address for sign-ups you do not trust.
- [File.io](https://file.io) — temporary file sharing; the file deletes after it is downloaded.
- [archive.ph](https://archive.ph) — save a snapshot of any webpage.
- [10 Minute Mail](https://10minutemail.com) — a disposable email address that lasts ten minutes.

## Music and focus

- [Radio Garden](https://radio.garden) — explore live radio stations around the world.
- [Every Noise at Once](https://everynoise.com) — discover music genres you have never heard of.
- [TuneFind](https://www.tunefind.com) — find the songs used in TV shows and movies.
- [Music for Programming](https://musicforprogramming.net) — focus music for long work sessions.
- [myNoise](https://mynoise.net) — custom background sounds you can tune.
- [Coffitivity](https://coffitivity.com) — café ambience for working.

## Developer tools

- [Phind](https://www.phind.com) — AI answers for developers, with sources.
- [Regex101](https://regex101.com) — test and debug regular expressions.
- [CodeBeautify](https://codebeautify.org) — format and validate code.
- [JSON Formatter](https://jsonformatter.org) — format and validate JSON.
- [ExplainShell](https://explainshell.com) — understand what a terminal command actually does.

## Everyday utilities

- [AlternativeTo](https://alternativeto.net) — find alternatives to any app.
- [JustWatch](https://justwatch.com) — find where to stream a movie or show.
- [SimilarSites](https://similarsites.com) — find websites similar to one you already use.
- [Raindrop.io](https://raindrop.io) — a bookmark manager that actually stays organised.
- [Downdetector](https://downdetector.com) — check whether a service is down for everyone.
- [TinEye](https://tineye.com) — reverse image search.
- [Fast.com](https://fast.com) — a quick internet speed test.
- [Smallpdf](https://smallpdf.com) — PDF tools: compress, convert, edit.
- [iLovePDF](https://ilovepdf.com) — merge, split and convert PDFs.

## A note before you bookmark all fifty

Most of these are free for personal use, but free tiers change and a few have paid plans for heavy use. Check the site itself before you build a workflow around it. And for the research tools in particular: they speed up finding papers, they do not replace reading them.
