## What has been announced

The University of Engineering and Technology (UET), Peshawar is bringing the HEC Need-Based Scholarship Programme for its students. The announcement, shared as a poster, says the university is expected to open the application process within the next two weeks, with an expected date of 20 November 2026.

UET Peshawar lists HEC Need-Based Scholarships among the financial-assistance schemes available to deserving students on a merit and affordability basis. That is the programme background; it is not the announcement of this opening.

## What is not yet confirmed

The 20 November 2026 date is an expected date, not a confirmed one. As checked on 4 November 2026, no official UET notice confirming the opening date was found on the university's website, and the poster itself gives no eligibility criteria, no list of required documents, no application link, and no deadline.

Nothing in this article should be read as confirming any of those details. Eligibility, documents, and the application process will only be known when UET publishes its official announcement.

## What students should do

Students are advised to stay alert and keep an eye on official university announcements for further updates on the application process, eligibility criteria, and required documents. Do not rely on forwarded posters or social media posts alone for the details that decide an application.

This article will be updated when the official UET notice appears. Until then, treat 20 November 2026 as the expected opening date only.
