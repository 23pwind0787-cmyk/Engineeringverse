## What it is

The GitHub Student Developer Pack is a free bundle for verified students: GitHub's own paid features while you are a student, plus partner offers you claim one by one from the pack page. It costs nothing to apply, and verification does not switch every benefit on at once — you claim each partner offer separately.

One thing it does not have: GitHub runs no referral programme for the pack. Telling a friend about it earns you nothing; your friend simply gets the same benefits if they qualify and verify with their own student proof.

## Who qualifies

According to GitHub's documentation, you qualify if you meet all four of these:

- You are enrolled in a degree- or diploma-granting programme — high school, college, university or homeschool.
- You can show current student status with documents.
- You have a personal GitHub account.
- You are at least 13 years old.

You do not need to be studying computer science. Any student at an accredited institution qualifies.

## What proof to prepare

GitHub accepts one of these as proof of current enrolment:

- A picture of your school ID card showing the current enrolment date.
- Your class schedule.
- Your transcript.
- An enrolment verification letter.

A school-issued email address also matters: if recent applicants from your school verified with an academic email, GitHub expects you to add and verify one on your GitHub account too. Add it under Settings, then Emails, before you apply. Photograph documents flat, in good light, with every date readable — blurry images and old dates are the usual reasons applications get rejected.

## How to apply

1. Create a personal GitHub account if you do not have one, and add and verify your university email under Settings, then Emails.
2. Open the Education benefits section of your GitHub settings and choose "Start an application".
3. Upload your proof document if asked.
4. Wait for the decision. Approval usually takes a few days, but can take longer at the start of a semester, when many students apply at once.

GitHub re-checks your student eligibility every month, so keep your proof current. The benefits last while you are a student.

## The offers worth claiming first

These are the offers listed on GitHub's pack page, checked on 10 November 2026. Partner offers change without notice, so check the pack page again before you plan around any one of them.

- **GitHub Pro** — free while you are a student.
- **GitHub Copilot Student** — listed on the pack page as available to verified students, with unlimited code completions and an allowance of GitHub AI Credits, plus limited chat and agent usage with auto model selection only. Availability of Copilot plans has changed before, so confirm it on the pack page when you apply.
- **Free domain** — Namecheap gives one year of registration on a .me domain, plus one SSL certificate free for one year. Name.com offers a free domain across more than 25 extensions, and .TECH offers one standard .TECH domain free for one year.
- **Microsoft Azure** — free access to more than 25 Azure cloud services plus $100 in Azure credit, for students aged 18 and over. No credit card required.
- **JetBrains** — a free subscription to professional IDEs such as IntelliJ IDEA and PyCharm, renewed annually.
- **Heroku** — $13 per month in credit for 24 months.
- **1Password** — free for 2 years, including 1Password Developer Tools.
- **Educative** — 6 months of free access to more than 70 practical courses.
- **DataCamp** — 3 months of free access.
- **Frontend Masters** — 6 months of free access to all courses and workshops.
- **MongoDB** — $50 in Atlas credits, plus access to MongoDB University including free certification valued at $150.
- **GitHub Codespaces** — free Pro level access.
- **GitHub Pages** — one site per GitHub account, plus unlimited project sites.
- **Stripe** — $25 in credits to offset fees.

## A UET example

This site's own domain is an example of the pack working. EngineeringVerse runs on engineeringverse.me, registered free for one year through the Namecheap offer in the pack after GitHub Education verification with a UET Peshawar email address (23pwind0787@uetpeshawar.edu.pk). The application was approved on 31 October 2026 and the benefits unlocked on 3 November 2026. The domain is free until 3 November 2027; after that it renews at Namecheap's normal price.

If you are a UET Peshawar student, the same route is open to you: apply with your university email and a current student ID or enrolment letter, and claim the offers you will actually use — the domain, Copilot, Azure credits and JetBrains are the ones most engineering students get value from first.
